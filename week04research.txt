There are 2 cases per severity, low, medium, and high.

Clients' reported security cases go through an intricate process from initiation to resolution, and the type of threat calls for 
its designated security department.threat intelligence department handles attribution, campaign correlation, checking whether a
case is part of a broader attack pattern. Secondary on medium/high, primary for recon-only cases.

HR/ insider risk departments handle the cases which are reported due to internal issues of their company or extended employee sectors. 
Cases where an employee or contractor is the vector: credential misuse, policy violations, data exfiltration by insiders, phishing 
compromise followed by account misuse. Needs pairing with IR so evidence handling isn't contaminated.

Data protection and compliance department is addressed when the security cases are related to data breach, credentials leak,
or invalid attempts to login. This department usually triggers the legal clock on breach cases.

Research on types of cyber cases on basis of degree of severity

High: 
Impact: Massive Corporate insecurity, severe financial loss, exposure of sensitive data causing operational destruction.
Response: Immediate (within 10 minutes)
Examples:
•	Ransomware, Worms and Malware Attacks on critical infrastructure (e.g.: hospitals)
•	Mass data breaches (containing financial records, SSNs, card payment data etc.)
•	Supply chain attacks (affecting software vendors and downstream to enterprise customers)
•	DDOS (Distributed denial of service attack) on ore infrastructure
•	Social engineering in reduced time frame
Medium: 
Impact: Destructs branched (smaller scale) business tasks, targeted phishing, unauthorized access etc. (but not causing catastrophic damage
Response:  Quick (under an hour or two)
Examples: 
•	Spear fishing (to specific employees deceiving them to give away sensitive information)
•	Stalker ware and targeted spyware
•	Pharming
•	Viruses
Low: 
Impact: low; generic spam and minor security policy issues
Response: Standard (under 12 hours)
Examples: 
•	Generic Spam and non-targeted phishing
•	Minor internal security policy violations (e.g: employee using unauthorized USB drive to steal work files)
•	Brute-Force authentication attacks (low-level)
•	Adware
