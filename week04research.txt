There are 2 cases per severity, low, medium, and high.

Clients' reported security cases go through an intricate process from initiation to resolution, and the type of threat calls for 
its designated security department.threat intelligence department handles attribution, campaign correlation, checking whether a
case is part of a broader attack pattern. Secondary on medium/high, primary for recon-only cases.

HR/ insider risk departments handle the cases which are reported due to internal issues of their company or extended employee sectors. 
Cases where an employee or contractor is the vector: credential misuse, policy violations, data exfiltration by insiders, phishing 
compromise followed by account misuse. Needs pairing with IR so evidence handling isn't contaminated.

Data protection and compliance department is addressed when the security cases are related to data breach, credentials leak,
or invalid attempts to login. This department usually triggers the legal clock on breach cases.
